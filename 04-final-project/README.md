# dm2008-SauShyh-Liong-AY2627-s1

For final project

Ideas:

1. Firefly Jar (batteries and solar charging)

The idea: Fireflies are like tiny rechargeable batteries. They glow when charged and fade when empty.

Phototransistor: Shine a torch on it and the fireflies charge up, like a solar panel in sunlight. Cover it and it becomes night, so they slowly drain.
Force sensor: Tap or press to shake the jar, which scatters the fireflies.
Ultrasonic: Bring your hand closer and the fireflies gather towards it, like moths to a lamp. Pull away and they drift off.
Over time: Fireflies have energy levels, can go dim, sleep, and wake up. A bright, well-fed swarm behaves differently from a tired one.
Look: Deep navy background with soft yellow-green glows. It has a clear EEE link (charge, energy, battery) without any jargon.

2. Echo Cave (sound bounces back)

The idea: Shout into a cave and the sound bounces back. The further the wall, the longer the echo takes.

Ultrasonic: Your hand is the cave wall. Distance sets how long each echo takes to return and how far the ripple travels on screen.
Force sensor: Press to “shout”. A harder press sends a louder pulse that travels further and lights up more of the cave.
Phototransistor: Controls the cave’s lamp. Bright light reveals the cave walls clearly, while darkness makes it murky and you have to rely on echoes.
Over time: Each echo leaves a fading glow on the walls, so the cave slowly maps itself as you explore. Creatures wake up when pinged too often.
Look: Sonar teal on black with glowing rings. It is also a neat nod to how your ultrasonic sensor actually works.

3. Light Painter

Each pendulum tip is a pen of light on a long-exposure canvas, like a camera shutter left open in a dark room. The trails persist and slowly fade, so the screen builds up a glowing drawing that no two runs repeat. Sliders control mass and length, which change the character of the drawing (long and light gives wide, whippy loops, while heavy and short gives tight, dense knots). The player drags the bobs with the mouse to set the starting pose and flicks them to add energy. A key press saves the current canvas as a poster, which gives your documentation a nice gallery of outputs.

States: Setup, Swinging, Chaotic, Settling (friction slowly calms it), Frozen artwork
Look: Additive blending, colour mapped to angular velocity, so fast sections burn white-hot and slow sections cool to deep blue or violet.
